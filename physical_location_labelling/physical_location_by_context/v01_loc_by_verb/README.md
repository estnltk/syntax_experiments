## v01 - finding physical locations by verbs

Trying to find physical locations by finding words in spatial cases that are several verbs' direct dependents. Tested removing words denoting time and adding a concreteness rating. Results had good quality but too little quantity.

* **base_data**: word lists for filtering out time words and phrasal verbs
* **code**: jupyter notebooks for automatically finding physical locations and time words
* **results**: lists of found physical locations
