Koha definitsioon:
A location usually answers the question “where”. It is a place or concept where something or someone is located. That place can be concrete (bank, table, Berlin), abstract (literature, soul, government), inanimate (journal, chair, wifi), alive (mother, Peter, dog).
Locations ARE NOT phrases that show time, state of being, owner, experiencer, instrument, manner OR are purely grammatical constructions. If the phrase can answer the question when, in what state, who, with what or how, then it is not a location.
